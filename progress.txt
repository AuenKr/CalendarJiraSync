
## Codebase Patterns
- Use `toLocaleDateString('en-CA')` or manual timezone offset adjustment for local YYYY-MM-DD strings to avoid UTC mismatches with Calendar dates.
- Google Calendar Event Bubble (popup) uses `[role="heading"]` for title, while Edit Page uses `input[aria-label="Add title"]`.
